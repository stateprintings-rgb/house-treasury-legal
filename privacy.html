HOUSE TREASURY · PRIVATE FINANCIAL OFFICE

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 20 October 2026

1. Purpose and scope

House Treasury is a private personal-finance application intended for use by its owner. It is not offered as a public consumer service. This policy describes how the application handles information when it is used.

2. Information handled by the app

House Treasury may handle information entered by the user, including account names, balances, debts, income, expenses, business figures, property figures, currencies, savings and charitable targets. If Open Banking is enabled, the app may also receive information made available by the user’s bank through the selected Open Banking provider, such as account identifiers, account names, currencies and balances.

3. Storage

House Treasury is designed primarily as a local application. Financial records and integration configuration used by the app are stored on the user’s device. Sensitive application credentials are placed in Android secure storage where supported. House Treasury does not operate its own analytics, advertising or profiling service.

4. Open Banking

Where the user elects to connect bank accounts, House Treasury communicates with Enable Banking and the user’s selected bank. Bank authentication is performed through the bank’s own authorisation flow. House Treasury does not ask the user to enter their online-banking password into the app.

The user controls which bank accounts are authorised and may revoke that access through the relevant bank or Open Banking provider. Open Banking access is subject to the provider’s and bank’s own privacy terms and retention rules.

5. Other external services

The app may contact third-party services to retrieve information such as foreign-exchange rates and precious-metal prices. Requests to those services may necessarily expose ordinary network information such as the device’s IP address. House Treasury does not sell personal information to advertisers.

6. Sharing

House Treasury does not intentionally disclose the user’s personal financial information to unrelated third parties. Information is transmitted to external services only where necessary for a feature the user chooses to use, such as Open Banking or market-data retrieval.

7. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect locally stored information, including secure device storage for sensitive values where available. No software or storage mechanism can guarantee absolute security, and the user remains responsible for protecting access to their device, bank accounts and any private application credentials.

8. Retention and deletion

Local House Treasury data remains on the device until the user edits, clears or removes it, or removes the application and its associated data. Data held by banks or third-party providers is governed by their respective policies and the user’s authorisations.

9. Contact

For privacy or data-protection matters, use the contact email supplied in the House Treasury Enable Banking application registration.
